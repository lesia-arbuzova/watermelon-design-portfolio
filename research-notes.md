# Portfolio research notes

The website uses principles observed across current portfolio galleries and case-study guidance, without reproducing any individual design.

## Principles applied

- Lead with a concise positioning statement and direct access to selected work.
- Curate a small set of strong cases instead of presenting every available asset.
- Give each featured case a short challenge, solution and deliverables structure.
- Use an editorial grid, generous whitespace and restrained interaction so the work stays primary.
- Keep navigation and contact information immediately available.
- Provide a responsive, bilingual experience rather than treating the HTML as a fixed slide deck.
